# Aura

A local voice assistant. Works without cloud.
Your data never leaves your device.

It helps you speak with the digital world — banking, government
services, doctors, communications — by voice.

## Who it is for

Elderly, blind, people with motor and cognitive impairments.
Anyone who struggles with the digital world.

## What it can do

- Voice input and output — offline, Russian
- Calls, messages, email — by voice
- Medications, doctor visits, reminders
- Smart home — lights, sockets, sensors, doorbell
- Weather, time, music, radio
- Emergency call, fall detection
- Reads notifications aloud, voice reply
- Speaks messages from Telegram, WhatsApp, VK, MAX
